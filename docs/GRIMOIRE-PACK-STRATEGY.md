### Price Architecture (Validated)

```
$1-$5      Digital PDF (commodity, high volume)
$5-$15     Digital bundle / printed paperback
$15-$50    Physical starter kit (THE SWEET SPOT)
$50-$83    Large/premium kit (7.7k reviews at $83.49)
$85-$145   Custom/personalized gift box
$110-$350  Premium limited edition
$400-$675  Collector/grand binding
```

### What Makes Kits Sell

**The #1 differentiator reviewers praise:**
> "I love that everything had instructions and paperwork to explain what it was for"

**Successful kits always include:**
1. Physical tools (candles, herbs, crystals)
2. **Education layer** (printed guide, correspondences)
3. Gift-ready packaging
4. Themed presentation
5. Mystery/rotation angle (drives repeat purchases)

### Charity Tie-In Data

| Source | Finding |
|--------|---------|
| **eBay academic study** (23M+ auctions) | Charity listings: **+6 to +14 percentage points** sale probability |
| **made4aid** (Etsy) | £16,220 raised for Doctors Without Borders |
| **Plank Hardware** (Etsy) | £29,000+ total charity raise |
| **Gingerful** | 1% to WWF → £2,000+ in six months |

**Verdict:** Charity is a **differentiator and trust signal**, not a pricing lever. Best on physical packs where the donation % is material enough to mention.

## Charity Program

### How It Works

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Donation** | 5% of net profit per sale |
| **Default charity** | National Alliance on Mental Illness (NAMI) |
| **Customer choice** | 4 pre-selected charities to choose from |
| **Transparency** | Monthly donation receipt published on website |
| **Etsy note** | Listed on physical product descriptions only |

### Pre-Selected Charities

| Charity | Focus | Why |
|---------|-------|-----|
| **NAMI** (default) | Mental health | Occult community overlaps heavily with mental health advocacy |
| **The Trevor Project** | LGBTQ+ youth | Large LGBTQ+ representation in witchcraft community |
| **Animal Rescue** | Animal welfare | "Cat witch" / familiar energy in community |
| **Environmental** | Climate/conservation | Green witchcraft movement |

### Etsy Policy Compliance

| Rule | Compliance |
|------|-----------|
| Physical products only | ✅ Digital downloads don't qualify |
| Seller donation | ✅ We donate, not Etsy |
| Disclose % clearly | ✅ 5% stated on listing |
| Name the charity | ✅ Charity named in description |
| No false claims | ✅ Real donations, real receipts |

### Marketing Copy

> "5% of every Ochema grimoire pack goes to charity. Choose your cause at checkout — or our default, NAMI (mental health), where we'll donate 5% of net proceeds. Because the occult community cares about each other."
