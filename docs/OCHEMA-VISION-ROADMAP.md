# Ochema — The Vision & Roadmap

> The wedge: Find old niche grimoires → read cleanly → AI assistance → save your version → print it.
> But premium store first. App later.

---

## The Wedge (The Killer Idea)

**Find a super old niche grimoire. Read it cleanly. AI reads it aloud. Save your annotated version. Order a personalized print.**

```
1. Find: Rare public domain grimoire (Sworn Book, Picatrix, etc.)
2. Read: Beautiful reader with proper typography
3. AI: Voice reads it aloud, like a bedtime story
4. Annotate: Add your own notes, highlights, commentary
5. Save: Your personalized version stored in Vault
6. Print: Order your annotated grimoire as a physical book
7. Share: Your annotated version can be public (if you want)
```

**The magic:** Each person becomes a scholar. Each person has a personal annotated grimoire. Each person can share their annotations with the community.
