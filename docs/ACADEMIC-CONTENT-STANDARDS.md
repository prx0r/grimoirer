# Ochema — Academic Content Standards

> Not clickbait. Not scam. Scholarly, premium, trustworthy.
> The audience trusts us because we take their practice seriously.

---

## The Principle

**We treat the occult the way a university treats literature.**

Not "5 MAGICAL SPELLS THAT WORK!!!"
But "The Greek Magical Papyri: Translation and Context"

Not "MANIFEST MONEY OVERNIGHT"
But "The Emerald Tablet: A Historical Reading"

Not "LUCID DREAM SECRETS REVEALED"
But "Artemidorus's Oneirocritica: Dream Interpretation in the Ancient World"

---

## Content Standards

### Voice
| We Are | We Are Not |
|--------|-----------|
| Scholarly | Academic-sounding but wrong |
| Precise | Vague and mystical |
| Honest about sources | Claiming secrets nobody knows |
| Respectful of traditions | Appropriating or mocking |
| Clear about what's proven | Overpromising results |
| Transparent about what we don't know | Pretending to be an authority on everything |

### Language Rules
| Instead of | Say |
|-----------|-----|
| "Ancient secret that works" | "Historical practice documented in [source]" |
| "Guaranteed results" | "Traditional method with documented use" |
| "Only the initiated know" | "This text was written for [audience]" |
| "Magical powers" | "Historical magical thinking" |
| "Summon your daimon" | "The Greek concept of daimon as inner guide" |
| "Cast this spell successfully" | "The ritual as described in [source]" |

### Source Citations
Every video includes:
1. Primary source (text, papyrus, manuscript)
2. Date of original composition
3. Translation used
4. Historical context
5. "This is a historical practice, not a promise of results"

## Channel 1: Daimon Dreams

### The Concept
Greek dream traditions — the daimon, dream incubation, oneirocriticism (dream interpretation). Academic but accessible. Not "woo-woo" — real historical material.

### Episode Structure (30-45 minutes)

```
[OPENING — 2 min]
"Tonight we're reading from [source], written in [date] by [author].
This is a historical text about [topic]. I'll explain what it meant
to the people who wrote it, and what it might mean to you."

[CONTEXT — 5 min]
Historical background. Who wrote this? When? What was happening?
What did "dreams" mean to this culture?

[THE TEXT — 15-20 min]
Read the actual passage (translated). Explain difficult concepts.
Provide modern parallels where appropriate.

[INTERPRETATION — 5 min]
What does this mean? How was it used? What are the scholarly debates?

[CLOSING — 2 min]
"This is a historical practice documented in [source]. Whether you
find meaning in it is up to you. Sleep well."
```

### Episode Structure (30-45 minutes)

```
[OPENING — 2 min]
"Today we're reading from [person]'s diary, written in [year].
[Person] was a [occupation] who [brief context]. This entry
describes [topic]."

[HISTORICAL CONTEXT — 5 min]
Who was this person? What was happening politically, scientifically,
spiritually? Why were they keeping a diary?

[THE DIARY ENTRY — 15-20 min]
Read the actual entry. Explain references. Provide modern parallels.

[WHAT WE KNOW AND DON'T KNOW — 5 min]
Be honest. Some of this is documented fact. Some is interpretation.
Some is legend. We distinguish between them.

[CLOSING — 2 min]
"This is a historical document. Whether you find meaning in it
is up to you. Sleep well."
```

## The Academic Framework

### Every Video Includes
1. **Primary source citation** — text, author, date
2. **Translation credit** — who translated it
3. **Historical context** — what was happening when this was written
4. **Scholarly debate** — "scholars disagree about..."
5. **Honest disclaimer** — "this is a historical practice, not a promise"

### What Makes Us Trustworthy
| Signal | How We Demonstrate It |
|--------|----------------------|
| **Sources** | Cite primary texts in description |
| **Dates** | Always give original composition date |
| **Translations** | Name the translator |
| **Context** | Explain historical background |
| **Honesty** | Distinguish fact from legend |
| **Respect** | Treat traditions seriously |
| **No promises** | "Historical practice" not "works guaranteed" |

### What We Never Do
- ❌ Claim secret knowledge nobody else has
- ❌ Promise specific results from rituals
- ❌ Appropriate closed religious traditions without context
- ❌ Use fear or scarcity tactics
- ❌ Hide our sources
- ❌ Pretend to be an authority on everything
- ❌ Sell "guaranteed" spell outcomes
