## The Real Question: One Quality or Tiers?

### Option A: One Quality (Standard)

**Pick one level and do it well.**

| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| Simpler to source | Can't upsell |
| One supplier relationship | Miss out on premium buyers |
| Consistent quality | Can't serve budget buyers |
| Easier to explain | Less market coverage |

**My recommendation:** Standard metal (brass + incense + linen). Not the cheapest, not the most expensive. Quality that feels premium without being pretentious.

**Cost:** ~$40 per kit
**Retail:** $50
**Margin:** $10 (20%)

---

### Option B: Two Tiers (Standard + Premium)

| Tier | Cost | Retail | Margin |
|------|------|--------|--------|
| Standard (brass) | $40 | $50 | $10 (20%) |
| Premium (silver) | $90 | $100 | $10 (10%) |

**Problem:** Premium margin is too thin. Silver costs too much.
