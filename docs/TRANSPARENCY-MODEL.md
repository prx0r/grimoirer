## The Honesty Principles

### 1. Show the real cost
Don't hide that the candle costs $0.50. Show it. Then explain why the kit costs $18.99.

### 2. Name the suppliers
"We source from AromaG's Botanica in Nashville" builds more trust than "premium essential oils."

### 3. Explain the margin
A 35-40% margin on physical products is fair. Show it. Don't pretend it's charity.

### 4. No false scarcity
"Only 3 left!" when we have 500 in production. Just say "in stock."

### 5. No fake urgency
"Sale ends tonight!" when the sale ends whenever. Just price it fairly from the start.

### 6. Show the alternatives
"If you want to make this yourself, here's where to buy the components for $3.40." Some people will. That's fine. They become advocates.
