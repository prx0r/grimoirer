## What it is (one line)

*Ars Brevis* is a mid-14th-c. Notoria-tradition treatise — composite layer in Agrippa/Turner alongside Version B — with **four experiment figures**, **three of which have contested identities**.

## Working titles (Castle descriptive method)

| Working title | Experiment | Our stack use |
|---------------|------------|---------------|
| Figure of memory (AGLA-style) | memory | optional nota after core Notoria stable |
| Figure of intellect (Phos Megalos-style) | intellect | pair with understanding general / grammar |
| Secrets of God (tau-cross style) | historical “secrets” | optional depth only |
| Master Albert (Regensburg) | historical secrets | optional |

## Practice rule

Same shell as Notoria — **faculty → nota → special → study → log**.  
Ars Brevis figures are **optional notae**, not a second path. Tea never required.

## When to add

After the 4-week Notoria grammar track is stable. Then open one figure as a nota for one experiment only.
